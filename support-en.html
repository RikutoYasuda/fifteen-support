App: FIFTEEN (iOS)

How to play

Choose one to five of your eight cards and play them with the dealer's table cards (two face up and one face down) to make sets whose values add up to 15. More fifteens score more; enough fifteens in one play make a FEVER, and fevers in a row build a chain that raises the multiplier. See "How to play" in the app for details.

FAQ

  • How do I remove ads? Buy "Remove Ads" in the app's Settings (a one-time purchase).
  • My purchase is gone after a new phone or reinstall. Sign in with the same Apple Account and press "Restore Purchases" in the app's Settings.
  • Where are my records and progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • My score is not on the leaderboard. Check that you are signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings. The daily leaderboards take the score through Chapter VIII, and a new day starts at 00:00 UTC.
