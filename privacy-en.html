Privacy

App: FIFTEEN (iOS)
Operator: Rikuto Yasuda
Effective: 2026-09-24

Information in the app

Your game progress, records and settings are stored on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync of the app's own. The app does not send your game information (hands, scores, what you played) to the developer.

Game Center (daily leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, you can post a score to the daily leaderboards. The app posts only the score of a daily run (through Chapter VIII), and only when you press the button to post it. The name shown on the leaderboards follows your Game Center settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy for how Game Center handles information.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, full-screen ads appear at breaks, such as the end of a run. The ads are served by Google AdMob, which handles information like the following to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language) and IP address
  • Ad views and taps, and your approximate region
  • Diagnostic information about how the app runs
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow "Allow Apps to Request to Track", to show ads that suit you

You can play without allowing tracking; you will then see ads that are not based on your activity. You can change this at any time in the iOS Settings. In regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK), the app shows a consent form, and you can change your choice in the app's settings.

If you buy "Remove Ads", no ads are shown and the app makes no ad requests to AdMob. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase

Apple processes the purchase and payment of "Remove Ads". The app only learns whether you have bought it; it never receives your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Device backups

iOS and iCloud backups are Apple services that follow the settings on your device. If a backup includes the app's data, Apple's terms and privacy information apply. The developer cannot manage backups or read records from them.

Contacting support

Write to ricton513@gmail.com. Your email may include your reply address, your message, and any name or attachment you add. Support email is received in Gmail and used to reply and look into problems. It is kept as long as needed for support, with no fixed retention period; to ask for deletion, write to the same address. Please do not send personal or sensitive information that support does not need.

This website

This site is a set of static pages served by GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics and no cookies. To serve the pages, GitHub processes access information such as visitors' IP addresses; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes to this page
